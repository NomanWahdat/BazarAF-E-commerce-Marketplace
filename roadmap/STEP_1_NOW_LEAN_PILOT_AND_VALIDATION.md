# Step 1: Now - Lean Pilot and Market Validation

## Purpose

The first step is to prove that BazarAF can work in the real Afghanistan market before spending large amounts of money on offices, warehouses, delivery fleets, or big marketing campaigns.

At this stage, your goal is not to look like a huge company. Your goal is to prove that customers will buy, sellers will cooperate, and orders can be delivered successfully.

## Why This Step Comes First

You currently have limited money for production hosting, marketing, office setup, delivery infrastructure, and warehouses. Because of this, the safest strategy is to start small and validate demand.

If you try to build everything at once, the project can fail because of high expenses before it gets enough users and orders.

## Main Objective

Launch a controlled marketplace pilot in one city, with selected sellers, limited categories, manual operations, and low-cost delivery partnerships.

## Recommended Pilot City

Start with one city only.

Recommended first city:

- Kabul

Why Kabul first:

- Larger customer base
- More sellers and shops
- Better delivery availability
- More internet users
- Better chance to find early adopters
- Easier to meet sellers and partners

## Product Scope for This Phase

Do not launch every possible feature. Focus only on what is needed for real buying and selling.

### Must-Have Features

- User signup and login
- Email/OTP verification
- Product browsing
- Product detail page
- Cart
- Cash on Delivery checkout
- Order tracking
- Seller application
- Admin seller approval
- Admin product approval
- Basic notifications
- Customer support contact information

### Features to Avoid for Now

- Own delivery fleet
- Warehouses
- Advanced payment systems
- Complex analytics dashboards
- Full mobile apps
- Expensive AI features
- Complex seller subscription systems
- Multi-city operations

## Category Strategy

Start with categories that are easy to sell and deliver.

Recommended early categories:

- Mobile accessories
- Electronics accessories
- Beauty and cosmetics
- Fashion accessories
- Small home items
- Books and stationery
- Computer accessories

Avoid at the start:

- Heavy furniture
- Fragile expensive items
- Fresh food
- Medicine
- Large appliances
- Products with complicated returns

## Seller Strategy

Start with a small number of trusted sellers.

Recommended target:

- 10 to 30 verified sellers
- 200 to 500 quality products

### Seller Selection Criteria

Choose sellers who:

- Have a real shop or known business location
- Can answer calls and messages quickly
- Have stable product availability
- Accept marketplace rules
- Can handle Cash on Delivery orders
- Agree to basic return conditions
- Provide real product photos and accurate prices

### Seller Onboarding Strategy

At this stage, do not expect all sellers to upload products themselves. Help them manually.

You can:

- Visit sellers physically
- Collect product photos through WhatsApp
- Upload products for them
- Teach them how orders work
- Create simple seller instructions
- Start with sellers who trust you personally

## Customer Strategy

Your first customers should be people who are easier to reach and educate.

Recommended early customer groups:

- University students
- Young professionals
- Social media users
- Friends and family networks
- Local community groups
- People already buying from Instagram/Facebook sellers

## Delivery Strategy

Do not build your own delivery system yet.

Use manual delivery coordination.

Options:

- Local courier partners
- Taxi/rickshaw delivery agreements
- Seller self-delivery
- Founder/team manual delivery for early orders
- City-area based delivery agents

### Delivery Process for Pilot

1. Customer places order.
2. Admin confirms order by phone or WhatsApp.
3. Seller confirms product availability.
4. Delivery person collects item.
5. Customer pays Cash on Delivery.
6. Delivery person returns seller payment minus commission/delivery fee.
7. Admin updates order status.

## Trust Strategy

Trust is more important than features in Afghanistan.

Add trust signals everywhere:

- Verified seller badge
- Clear seller information
- Real product photos
- Return policy
- Cash on Delivery message
- WhatsApp/phone support
- Order confirmation call
- Product review system
- Clear delivery time estimate

## Marketing Strategy for This Phase

Spend little or no money.

Use organic and direct marketing.

### Low-Cost Marketing Channels

- Facebook pages
- Instagram reels
- TikTok videos
- WhatsApp groups
- University groups
- Seller social media pages
- Referral from friends
- Local shop posters
- Short demo videos

### Message to Customers

Focus on trust and convenience:

- Buy from verified Afghan sellers
- Pay when you receive your order
- Easy order tracking
- Support in Dari/Pashto
- Local marketplace for Afghanistan

## Operational Strategy

Manual operations are acceptable in this phase.

Track everything in simple tools:

- Google Sheets
- WhatsApp groups
- Admin dashboard
- Phone call logs
- Delivery status sheet

### Daily Operations Checklist

- Check new seller applications
- Approve/reject products
- Confirm new orders
- Call customers for confirmation
- Confirm seller stock
- Assign delivery
- Update order statuses
- Follow up after delivery
- Record complaints and returns

## Metrics to Track

This phase is about learning.

Track:

- Number of registered users
- Number of verified users
- Number of sellers contacted
- Number of approved sellers
- Number of products listed
- Number of orders placed
- Number of successful deliveries
- Number of cancelled orders
- Customer complaints
- Repeat customers
- Average delivery time
- Most popular categories
- Seller response time

## Success Criteria

You can move to Step 2 if you achieve some of these:

- 10+ verified sellers
- 200+ products
- 50+ real customer registrations
- 20+ real orders
- 60%+ successful delivery rate
- Customers show willingness to buy again
- Sellers want to continue using the platform
- You understand the biggest operational problems

## Main Risks

- Sellers do not update stock
- Customers place fake orders
- Delivery fails because of wrong address
- Product quality mismatch
- Sellers delay response
- Customers do not trust online buying
- OTP/email issues reduce signup completion

## Risk Reduction Strategy

- Confirm every order by phone
- Start with trusted sellers only
- Use Cash on Delivery
- Require real product photos
- Keep delivery area small
- Use simple return rules
- Keep customer support active
- Verify phone numbers where possible

## What Not to Do Yet

Do not:

- Rent an expensive office
- Hire a large team
- Build a warehouse
- Buy delivery vehicles
- Spend heavily on ads
- Launch nationwide
- Promise same-day delivery everywhere
- Add too many product categories

## Immediate Action Plan

### Week 1

- Finalize local deployment
- Fix all critical auth/email issues
- Prepare seller agreement
- Prepare return/refund policy
- Choose first 3 to 5 categories

### Week 2

- Contact 20 to 30 sellers
- Approve first 10 sellers
- Upload 100 to 200 products
- Prepare customer support number
- Test checkout and order flow

### Week 3

- Start soft launch with friends, students, and social media groups
- Process first real orders manually
- Collect feedback
- Fix operational issues

### Week 4

- Review metrics
- Improve seller onboarding
- Improve delivery process
- Prepare early traction report

## Final Goal of Step 1

Prove that BazarAF can generate real orders in one city with limited money and manual operations.
