# Step 9: Create a Low-Cost Marketing Strategy

## Main Goal

Grow awareness and users without spending large amounts of money on advertising.

## Why This Step Matters

You mentioned that marketing budget is limited. This means BazarAF must use creative, low-cost, trust-based marketing instead of expensive campaigns.

## Marketing Principle

In the early stage, marketing should prove demand, not just create noise.

Focus on:

- Real orders
- Trust
- Seller promotion
- Customer referrals
- Community visibility
- Local social media presence

## Brand Positioning

BazarAF should be positioned as:

Afghanistan’s trusted online marketplace where customers can buy from verified local sellers and pay when they receive the product.

## Main Marketing Messages

Use simple messages:

- Buy safely from verified sellers.
- Pay when your order arrives.
- Shop online in Afghanistan with confidence.
- Support Afghan businesses.
- Easy ordering, local support.
- Products from trusted local shops.

## Social Media Strategy

Use platforms popular in Afghanistan.

Recommended:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts
- WhatsApp

### Content Ideas

Post:

- Product videos
- Seller stories
- Customer testimonials
- How to order videos
- Cash on Delivery explanation
- Behind-the-scenes delivery
- Weekly deals
- New seller announcements
- Trust and safety posts

## TikTok and Reels Strategy

Short videos can work well with low budget.

Video ideas:

- How to order from BazarAF
- Top 5 products under a certain price
- Seller of the week
- Customer receives order
- COD explained
- Product unboxing
- Before/after seller online transformation

## WhatsApp Marketing Strategy

WhatsApp is powerful but should not be spammy.

Use:

- Broadcast list for deals
- Customer support
- Seller communication
- Order updates
- Referral sharing

Rules:

- Get permission where possible.
- Send useful offers.
- Do not send too many messages.
- Keep messages short.

## Referral Strategy

Referral is important when money is limited.

Start manually.

Possible rewards:

- Discount on next order
- Free delivery
- Small wallet credit later
- Special coupon
- Seller discount

Track referrals using:

- Referral code
- Phone number
- Manual note
- Simple form

## Seller-Led Marketing

Let sellers help market the platform.

Give sellers:

- Store links
- Product links
- Social media templates
- BazarAF verified seller badge
- Promotional banners

Sellers can share these with their own audience.

## Micro-Influencer Strategy

Use small influencers instead of expensive celebrities.

Choose influencers who:

- Have real local followers
- Are affordable
- Are trusted
- Can explain the platform clearly
- Match your category audience

Offer:

- Free product
- Commission per order
- Small fixed payment
- Referral code partnership

## Community Marketing

Target communities:

- Universities
- Student groups
- Local markets
- Women entrepreneur groups
- Tech communities
- Office workers

Actions:

- Demo the website
- Share flyers/posters
- Offer first-order discounts
- Create student ambassador program
- Partner with small events

## Content Calendar

Post consistently.

Example weekly plan:

- Monday: Product deal
- Tuesday: Seller story
- Wednesday: How to order tutorial
- Thursday: Customer review
- Friday: New arrivals
- Saturday: Trust/safety message
- Sunday: Weekly summary

## Paid Ads Strategy

Use paid ads only after organic testing.

Start small:

- Test $5 to $10 campaigns if possible.
- Promote best-selling products.
- Target one city only.
- Track cost per order.

Do not spend heavily until you know what converts.

## Marketing Metrics

Track:

- Website visits
- Signup count
- Product views
- Add to cart count
- Orders from each channel
- Cost per customer
- Referral orders
- Social media engagement
- Repeat customers

## Mistakes to Avoid

Do not:

- Spend all money on ads.
- Market before sellers/products are ready.
- Promise things you cannot deliver.
- Use fake discounts.
- Ignore customer messages.
- Post inconsistently.

## Final Output of This Step

At the end of this step, BazarAF should have a low-cost marketing engine that brings early users, builds trust, and supports seller growth.
