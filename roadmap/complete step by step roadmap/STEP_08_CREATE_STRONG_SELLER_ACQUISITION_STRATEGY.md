# Step 8: Create a Strong Seller Acquisition Strategy

## Main Goal

Bring reliable sellers onto BazarAF so customers can find enough good products to buy.

## Why This Step Matters

A marketplace needs both buyers and sellers. If customers visit the platform and see few products, bad photos, wrong prices, or unreliable sellers, they will not return.

Seller quality is more important than seller quantity in the early stage.

## Ideal Early Sellers

Start with sellers who:

- Have real products
- Have stable stock
- Have good reputation
- Can respond quickly
- Accept Cash on Delivery
- Accept marketplace rules
- Can handle returns when needed
- Are interested in online growth

## Seller Categories to Target First

Good early seller types:

- Mobile accessory shops
- Cosmetics shops
- Electronics accessory shops
- Clothing boutiques
- Stationery shops
- Small home goods shops
- Instagram/Facebook sellers
- Women-owned home businesses

## Seller Value Proposition

Explain clearly why sellers should join.

Tell sellers:

- You can reach more customers online.
- You do not need to build your own website.
- BazarAF helps with orders and visibility.
- You only pay commission when you sell.
- Your shop becomes more professional.
- You can receive online customers without high marketing cost.

## Seller Acquisition Channels

Use direct outreach first.

Channels:

- Visit local markets
- Contact Instagram sellers
- Contact Facebook sellers
- Ask friends for shop introductions
- Use WhatsApp business groups
- Contact wholesalers
- Attend local business events if available

## Seller Onboarding Process

Create a standard process.

Steps:

1. Identify seller.
2. Contact seller.
3. Explain BazarAF.
4. Collect seller details.
5. Verify seller information.
6. Create seller account.
7. Add first products.
8. Explain order process.
9. Monitor first 5 orders.
10. Give feedback and improve.

## Seller Documents and Information

Collect:

- Seller name
- Shop name
- Phone number
- Email if available
- Shop address
- Product categories
- Payment/payout method
- Return agreement
- Identity/business proof if possible

## Product Upload Support

Early sellers may not upload products themselves.

Help them by:

- Taking product photos
- Writing product titles
- Creating descriptions
- Setting categories
- Adding prices
- Teaching dashboard basics

## Commission Strategy

Do not start with high commission.

Possible early strategy:

- 0% commission for first month
- Low commission during pilot
- Category-based commission later
- Higher commission only after value is proven

Example:

- Accessories: 8% to 12%
- Electronics: 3% to 7%
- Fashion: 10% to 15%
- Beauty: 8% to 15%

## Seller Retention Strategy

Keep good sellers active.

Offer:

- Featured product opportunities
- Seller performance reports
- Fast support
- Clear payout schedule
- Training
- Promotional campaigns
- Trusted seller badges

## Seller Performance Metrics

Track:

- Response time
- Stock availability
- Order acceptance rate
- Cancellation rate
- Product complaint rate
- Customer rating
- Return rate
- Delivery preparation time

## Seller Penalty Rules

Create fair rules.

Penalize:

- Fake products
- Wrong prices
- Repeated stock failure
- Poor packaging
- Late response
- Bad customer behavior

Possible actions:

- Warning
- Product removal
- Lower visibility
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent removal

## Seller Education

Create simple training materials.

Topics:

- How BazarAF works
- How orders are confirmed
- How COD works
- How returns work
- How to prepare product
- How to update stock
- How to improve product photos

## Mistakes to Avoid

Do not:

- Approve every seller.
- Focus only on seller count.
- Ignore product quality.
- Set commission too high early.
- Expect all sellers to understand e-commerce immediately.
- Delay seller payouts.

## Final Output of This Step

At the end of this step, BazarAF should have a growing base of reliable sellers with quality products and clear operating rules.
