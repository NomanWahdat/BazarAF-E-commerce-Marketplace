# Step 13: Build Strategic Partnerships

## Main Goal

Use partnerships to grow faster without paying for everything yourself.

## Why This Matters

BazarAF cannot build delivery, payments, marketing, and seller networks alone at the beginning.

## Partnership Types

- Delivery partners
- Payment providers
- Local sellers and wholesalers
- Universities
- Influencers/media pages
- NGOs/startup programs
- Telecom companies
- Business associations

## Partnership Strategy

- Start with small practical partnerships.
- Offer mutual benefit.
- Keep agreements simple and written.
- Test partnership performance before scaling.

## Priority Partnerships Now

1. Delivery partners
2. Trusted local sellers
3. Small influencers
4. Startup/entrepreneurship programs
5. Wholesale product suppliers

## Metrics

- Orders from partners
- Seller referrals
- Delivery success rate
- Partnership cost
- New users from campaigns

## Final Goal

Reduce costs and increase growth by working with partners instead of building everything alone.
