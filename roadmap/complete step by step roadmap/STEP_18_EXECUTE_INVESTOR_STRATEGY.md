# Step 18: Execute Investor Strategy

## Main Goal

Approach the right investors with proof, confidence, and a clear funding plan.

## Investor Targets

- Afghan business owners
- Afghan diaspora investors
- Startup accelerators
- NGOs supporting digital economy
- Telecom/payment companies
- Wholesalers and retail groups

## Preparation Before Contact

Have ready:

- Pitch deck
- One-page summary
- Financial model
- Product demo
- Traction report
- Funding ask
- Use of funds plan

## Outreach Strategy

1. Build a list of 50 potential investors/partners.
2. Ask for warm introductions.
3. Send short messages.
4. Schedule calls.
5. Show product demo.
6. Ask for advice first.
7. Discuss investment after interest is clear.

## Funding Use

Use investment for:

- Operations
- Seller acquisition
- Customer support
- Delivery partnerships
- Marketing tests
- Technical improvements
- Legal setup

## Mistakes to Avoid

- Giving too much equity early
- Accepting investors with bad terms
- Asking for money without metrics
- Spending investment on office/status instead of growth

## Final Goal

Secure funding or strategic support to continue growth without financial pressure.
