# Step 11: Improve Product Quality and Fraud Prevention

## Main Goal

Protect customers from fake products, bad sellers, fake orders, and delivery/payment fraud.

## Why This Matters

One bad seller or fake product can damage BazarAF's reputation. Trust is the foundation of the marketplace.

## Key Strategies

- Verify sellers before approval.
- Review products before publishing.
- Require real product photos.
- Track seller cancellation and complaint rates.
- Confirm high-risk orders by phone.
- Monitor repeated fake orders.
- Create a report product/seller option.

## Product Approval Checklist

- Clear title
- Real price
- Correct category
- Good images
- Honest description
- Stock confirmed
- No fake brand claims

## Fraud Types to Watch

- Fake customer orders
- Sellers listing unavailable products
- Price changes after order
- Fake discounts
- Poor-quality products
- Delivery cash mismatch

## Metrics

- Complaint rate
- Return rate
- Fake order rate
- Seller cancellation rate
- Product rejection rate

## Final Goal

Create a safer marketplace where customers trust that listed products and sellers are real.
