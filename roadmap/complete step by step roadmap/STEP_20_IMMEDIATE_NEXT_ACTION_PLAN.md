# Step 20: Immediate Next Action Plan

## Main Goal

Turn the roadmap into practical actions for the next 30 to 90 days.

## First 30 Days

Focus:

- Fix production issues
- Finalize email/OTP verification
- Prepare Railway/Vercel deployment
- Select pilot city
- Choose first categories
- Contact first sellers
- Prepare seller agreement
- Prepare return/refund policy
- Test order flow

## Days 31 to 60

Focus:

- Onboard 10 to 30 sellers
- Upload 200 to 500 products
- Start soft launch
- Process real orders manually
- Track delivery success
- Collect customer feedback
- Build traction report
- Improve support process

## Days 61 to 90

Focus:

- Improve seller quality
- Improve delivery coordination
- Start low-cost marketing
- Build investor pitch deck
- Prepare financial model
- Contact early investors/partners
- Improve product based on real feedback

## Weekly Routine

Every week:

- Review orders
- Review complaints
- Review seller performance
- Review delivery issues
- Review costs
- Improve one product/operation issue
- Record metrics

## What Not to Do Now

Do not:

- Build warehouse
- Buy delivery vehicles
- Rent expensive office
- Hire large team
- Launch nationwide
- Spend heavily on ads

## Final Goal

Move from project development into real marketplace validation with users, sellers, orders, metrics, and investor readiness.
