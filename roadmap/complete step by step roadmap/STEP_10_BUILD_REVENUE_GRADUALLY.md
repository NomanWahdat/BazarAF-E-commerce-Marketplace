# Step 10: Build Revenue Gradually

## Main Goal

Create sustainable revenue streams for BazarAF without hurting early growth or seller trust.

## Why This Step Matters

BazarAF needs money to survive, pay hosting, support staff, marketing, operations, delivery coordination, and future growth. But charging too much too early can push sellers away.

The strategy is to build revenue gradually.

## Revenue Principle

At the beginning, focus more on growth and trust than profit.

Revenue should increase as BazarAF provides more value to sellers and customers.

## Revenue Stream 1: Commission Per Order

This is the main marketplace revenue model.

BazarAF takes a percentage from each successful order.

### Early Commission Strategy

Start low.

Options:

- 0% commission during first pilot month
- Low fixed commission after pilot
- Category-based commission later

Example category commission:

- Electronics: 3% to 7%
- Mobile accessories: 8% to 12%
- Fashion: 10% to 15%
- Beauty: 8% to 15%
- Home goods: 8% to 12%

## Revenue Stream 2: Delivery Fee Margin

If delivery costs 80 AFN and customer pays 100 AFN, BazarAF earns 20 AFN.

Be careful:

- Do not make delivery too expensive.
- Keep delivery pricing clear.
- Avoid hidden fees.
- Use free delivery only for promotions or high-value orders.

## Revenue Stream 3: Featured Products

Sellers can pay to promote products.

Examples:

- Homepage featured product
- Category top listing
- Banner placement
- Deal of the week

Start this only after platform has traffic.

## Revenue Stream 4: Seller Subscription Plans

Later, sellers can pay monthly for extra tools.

Possible plans:

### Free Plan

- Basic listing
- Standard commission
- Limited promotion

### Pro Seller Plan

- More product listings
- Better dashboard
- Featured seller badge
- Promotional tools
- Lower commission option

### Premium Seller Plan

- Homepage promotion
- Advanced analytics
- Priority support
- Campaign participation

Do not start subscriptions too early.

## Revenue Stream 5: Advertising

When traffic grows, sellers and brands can advertise.

Ad types:

- Homepage banner
- Category banner
- Sponsored search result
- Sponsored product
- Newsletter/WhatsApp promotion

Only introduce ads after user trust is strong.

## Revenue Stream 6: B2B Bulk Orders

BazarAF can later support bulk orders for:

- Offices
- Schools
- Universities
- NGOs
- Small businesses

Products:

- Stationery
- Office supplies
- Electronics accessories
- Cleaning supplies
- Uniforms

B2B can generate larger order values.

## Revenue Stream 7: Fulfillment and Warehousing Fees

Later, when BazarAF has storage/warehouse support, sellers can pay for:

- Storage
- Packing
- Fast delivery
- Inventory handling
- Fulfillment service

This should only come after strong order volume.

## Revenue Stream 8: Private Label Products

Long term, BazarAF can sell its own branded products.

Examples:

- Phone accessories
- Stationery
- Basic home goods

This is high risk and should come much later.

## Pricing Strategy

Keep pricing transparent.

Avoid:

- Hidden fees
- Sudden commission increases
- Complicated seller charges
- Charging before value is proven

Use:

- Clear commission table
- Written seller agreement
- Monthly seller statement
- Simple payout records

## Revenue Tracking

Track every month:

- GMV
- Commission revenue
- Delivery revenue
- Ad revenue
- Subscription revenue
- Refunds
- Operational cost
- Net profit/loss

## Seller Communication

When introducing charges, explain value.

Tell sellers:

- BazarAF brings customers.
- BazarAF manages online visibility.
- BazarAF supports order coordination.
- BazarAF builds trust with customers.
- Commission is charged only when sales happen.

## Customer Pricing Strategy

Keep customer costs reasonable.

Customers should clearly see:

- Product price
- Delivery fee
- Total amount
- COD payment amount

No surprise charges at delivery.

## Success Criteria

This step is successful if:

- BazarAF earns revenue from real orders.
- Sellers accept commission.
- Customers continue buying.
- Revenue helps cover basic costs.
- You can show investors a clear business model.

## Mistakes to Avoid

Do not:

- Charge high commission too early.
- Add hidden fees.
- Sell ads before having traffic.
- Push subscriptions before sellers see value.
- Offer free delivery without knowing the cost.
- Ignore profit margins.

## Final Output of This Step

At the end of this step, BazarAF should have a clear revenue model that starts small, grows with marketplace value, and supports long-term sustainability.
