# Step 3: Reduce Production and Maintenance Cost

## Main Goal

Keep BazarAF alive and operational with the lowest possible monthly cost until revenue or investment is available.

## Why This Step Matters

One of your biggest problems is limited money for production deployment, maintenance, marketing, office, and operations. If monthly costs become too high early, the project may stop before it gets real traction.

The strategy is to stay lean.

## Cost Control Principle

Spend money only on things that directly help you get:

- Real users
- Real sellers
- Real orders
- Trust
- Investor proof

Avoid spending on things that only make the company look bigger but do not generate orders.

## Hosting Strategy

Use affordable hosting at the beginning.

Recommended approach:

- Use Railway/Render/Fly.io for backend if affordable.
- Use Vercel/Netlify for frontend.
- Use managed PostgreSQL only when needed.
- Avoid expensive cloud setups at the start.
- Monitor usage carefully.

## Database Strategy

For production, use PostgreSQL.

But control cost by:

- Starting with small managed database plan
- Cleaning unused data
- Avoiding unnecessary logs in database
- Backing up important data
- Monitoring storage usage

## Media Storage Strategy

Product images and seller documents can become expensive if unmanaged.

Use:

- Compressed images
- Cloudflare R2 or S3-compatible low-cost storage
- Image size limits
- File type validation
- Remove unused uploads

Avoid storing large original files forever unless needed.

## Development Strategy

Avoid overbuilding.

Do not build complex features until there is real need.

Build first:

- Reliable signup/login
- Seller approval
- Product listing
- Order checkout
- Admin operations
- Email/OTP
- Customer support tools

Delay:

- AI recommendations
- Advanced analytics
- Full warehouse system
- Own delivery app
- Complex loyalty system
- Multi-country support

## Team Cost Strategy

Keep the team small.

Early team can include:

- Founder/business lead
- Developer/technical lead
- Seller onboarding person
- Customer support/operations person

Use part-time help where possible.

Avoid hiring a large team before stable orders.

## Office Strategy

Do not rent an expensive physical office at the beginning.

Alternatives:

- Work remotely
- Use coworking space occasionally
- Meet sellers at their shops
- Use a small shared office only when required
- Use WhatsApp/phone for support

A physical office can come later when you need:

- Investor meetings
- Team operations
- Customer trust center
- Seller onboarding center

## Marketing Cost Strategy

Do not start with expensive paid ads.

Use low-cost marketing:

- Organic social media
- Seller sharing
- Referral program
- WhatsApp groups
- University communities
- Small influencers
- Customer testimonials

Paid ads should be tested with small budgets only.

## Tooling Strategy

Use free or low-cost tools:

- Google Sheets
- Google Drive
- WhatsApp Business
- Trello
- Notion
- Canva
- Free analytics tools
- GitHub

Only pay for tools when they save significant time or directly increase revenue.

## Monthly Cost Tracking

Create a monthly cost sheet.

Track:

- Hosting
- Database
- Domain
- Email/tools
- Marketing
- Salaries
- Delivery losses
- Office/admin
- Legal/accounting
- Miscellaneous

## Cost Reduction Checklist

Every month ask:

- Is this cost necessary?
- Does it help generate orders?
- Does it improve trust?
- Does it help investor proof?
- Can we replace it with a cheaper option?
- Can we delay it?

## Success Criteria

This step is successful if:

- Monthly cost is predictable.
- The platform stays online.
- You can operate without large funding.
- You are not forced to stop because of hosting or office expenses.
- You can invest limited money into seller/customer growth.

## Mistakes to Avoid

Do not:

- Rent an office too early.
- Use expensive cloud services unnecessarily.
- Hire too many employees.
- Build complex features before demand.
- Spend heavily on ads without tracking results.
- Buy equipment before operations require it.

## Final Output of This Step

At the end of this step, BazarAF should have a lean cost structure that allows the project to survive long enough to gain traction and attract investment.
