# Step 12: Prepare Legal and Business Setup

## Main Goal

Make BazarAF legally and professionally ready for investors, sellers, partners, and customers.

## Why This Matters

Investors and partners will take the project more seriously if the business has clear legal structure and policies.

## Key Documents Needed

- Business registration plan
- Founder agreement
- Seller agreement
- Terms and conditions
- Privacy policy
- Return/refund policy
- Delivery partner agreement
- Employment/contractor agreements

## Business Setup Priorities

1. Define ownership between founders.
2. Register the business when financially possible.
3. Open business bank/payment accounts when available.
4. Create basic accounting records.
5. Keep all seller and partner agreements documented.

## Legal Risks

- Seller disputes
- Customer refund complaints
- Tax issues
- Founder ownership conflicts
- Data/privacy problems
- Delivery partner liability

## Final Goal

Build a business foundation that can safely accept investment and operate professionally.
