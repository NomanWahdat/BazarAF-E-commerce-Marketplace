# Step 17: Build a Lean Team First

## Main Goal

Create a small, effective team that can operate the marketplace without high salary costs.

## Why This Matters

A large team too early will increase expenses before revenue is stable.

## Early Team Roles

### Founder or CEO

- Vision
- Investor relations
- Partnerships
- Business decisions

### Technical Lead

- Website/backend/frontend
- Bug fixing
- Deployment
- Product improvements

### Seller Acquisition Person

- Contact sellers
- Onboard shops
- Collect product information
- Maintain seller relationships

### Operations Coordinator

- Confirm orders
- Coordinate delivery
- Track payments
- Update order statuses

### Customer Support Person

- Answer calls/messages
- Handle complaints
- Follow up orders
- Support verification issues

## Hiring Strategy

- Start with part-time help.
- Use commission where possible.
- Hire only when workload is proven.
- Define clear responsibilities.

## Final Goal

Run BazarAF with a small team that can handle pilot and early growth efficiently.
