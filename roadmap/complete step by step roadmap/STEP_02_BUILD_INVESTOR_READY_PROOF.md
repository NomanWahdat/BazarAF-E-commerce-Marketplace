# Step 2: Build Investor-Ready Proof

## Main Goal

Prepare real evidence that BazarAF is more than just an idea or software project. Investors need proof that the market wants it, sellers need it, and the team can execute it.

## Why This Step Matters

You need investment to handle production costs, marketing, operations, office setup, delivery systems, and future warehouses. But investors will not invest only because the idea is good.

They need proof.

## What Investors Want to See

Investors usually look for:

- Real product
- Real users
- Real sellers
- Real orders
- Market opportunity
- Strong founder commitment
- Clear business model
- Growth potential
- Ability to execute with limited resources

## Evidence You Should Collect

### Product Evidence

Prepare:

- Live website link
- Backend/API working proof
- Admin dashboard screenshots
- Seller application flow
- Customer order flow
- OTP verification flow
- Google login flow
- Product browsing flow

### Market Evidence

Collect:

- Number of people interested
- Customer feedback
- Seller feedback
- Common problems people face when buying online
- Common problems sellers face when selling online
- Afghanistan e-commerce opportunity notes

### Traction Evidence

Track:

- Registered users
- Verified users
- Active users
- Sellers contacted
- Sellers onboarded
- Products listed
- Orders placed
- Orders delivered
- Repeat customers
- Gross Merchandise Value
- Revenue or commission earned

## Build a Traction Report

Create a simple monthly report.

Include:

- Month name
- Number of users
- Number of sellers
- Number of products
- Number of orders
- Delivery success rate
- Cancellation rate
- Customer feedback
- Seller feedback
- Problems discovered
- Improvements made

## Build a Pitch Deck

Prepare a professional pitch deck.

Recommended slides:

1. Vision
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Why Afghanistan needs this
5. Product demo
6. Market size
7. Business model
8. Traction
9. Go-to-market strategy
10. Competition
11. Team
12. Financial projection
13. Funding ask
14. Use of funds
15. Roadmap

## Build a Financial Model

Create a simple spreadsheet.

Include:

- Monthly orders
- Average order value
- Commission percentage
- Delivery revenue
- Seller subscription revenue later
- Marketing cost
- Hosting cost
- Team salaries
- Delivery cost
- Office/admin cost
- Monthly profit/loss
- Funding runway

## Investor Demo Strategy

Your demo should be short and practical.

Show:

- Customer signup
- Product browsing
- Add to cart
- Checkout with Cash on Delivery
- Order tracking
- Seller application
- Admin approval
- Email/OTP verification

Do not spend too much time explaining technical details unless the investor asks.

## Use of Funds Strategy

Be clear about how investment will be used.

Recommended use:

- Operations and customer support
- Seller acquisition
- Low-cost marketing
- Hosting and infrastructure
- Delivery partnerships
- Legal setup
- Product improvements

Avoid saying most money will go to:

- Expensive office decoration
- Large salaries
- Warehouses immediately
- Delivery vehicles immediately
- Uncontrolled advertising

## Success Criteria

This step is successful if you have:

- Pitch deck
- Financial model
- Product demo
- Traction report
- Clear funding request
- Clear roadmap
- List of target investors

## Mistakes to Avoid

Do not:

- Approach investors with only an idea.
- Hide problems.
- Give unrealistic numbers.
- Ask for too much money without a clear plan.
- Offer too much equity too early.
- Focus only on technology and ignore operations.

## Final Output of This Step

At the end of this step, you should be ready to confidently present BazarAF to investors, business owners, startup programs, and strategic partners.
