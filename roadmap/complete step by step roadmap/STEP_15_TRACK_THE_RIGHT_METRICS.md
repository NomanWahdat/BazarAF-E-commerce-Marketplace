# Step 15: Track the Right Metrics

## Main Goal

Use data to make decisions and prove growth to investors.

## Why This Matters

Without metrics, you cannot know what is working or convince investors that the project is growing.

## Core Metrics

- Registered users
- Verified users
- Active users
- Approved sellers
- Active sellers
- Products listed
- Orders placed
- Orders delivered
- Orders cancelled
- Repeat customers
- Gross Merchandise Value
- Revenue

## Operational Metrics

- Delivery success rate
- Average delivery time
- Seller response time
- Product rejection rate
- Customer complaint rate
- Refund/return rate

## Marketing Metrics

- Traffic source
- Social media engagement
- Referral orders
- Cost per customer
- Conversion rate

## Investor Metrics

- Monthly growth
- GMV
- Revenue
- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer acquisition cost
- Delivery success rate

## Final Goal

Create a simple weekly/monthly dashboard that shows real progress and problems.
