# Step 16: Prepare for Risks You Did Not Mention

## Main Goal

Identify and prepare for hidden risks before they damage the business.

## Key Risk Areas

### Technical Risks

- Website downtime
- Slow performance
- Data loss
- Security breaches
- Payment/email failures

### Operational Risks

- Failed deliveries
- Wrong addresses
- Seller delays
- Poor support response
- Product return disputes

### Financial Risks

- Cash on Delivery loss
- Fraud orders
- High marketing cost
- Delivery losses
- Uncontrolled monthly expenses

### Legal Risks

- Unclear seller agreements
- Customer disputes
- Tax/registration issues
- Founder ownership issues

### Reputation Risks

- Fake sellers
- Bad products
- Public complaints
- Delayed refunds

## Risk Strategy

- Keep written processes.
- Track complaints.
- Verify sellers.
- Back up data.
- Limit COD risk.
- Keep transparent policies.
- Respond quickly to customers.

## Final Goal

Build BazarAF so it can survive problems without losing trust or control.
